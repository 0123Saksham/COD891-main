About this atlas

A causal knowledge graph of agricultural water-stress in India, consolidated from 10 research papers. Each node is a measurable variable; each directed edge is a causal mechanism documented in the literature.

How to read it

  • Drag nodes in 3D · scroll to zoom · right-drag to pan.
  • Click a node to open its dossier and trace its full causal chain (upstream → bright gold, downstream → mint).
  • Click an edge to inspect the mechanism, conditions and supporting papers.
  • Click empty space to clear the highlight.

Encoding

  • Node color: role or cluster family (toggle in sidebar).
  • Node size: paper count + degree.
  • Edge thickness: causal strength (strong / moderate / weak).
  • Edge hue: direction (positive · negative · categorical).
  • cross-stressor edges link different stress clusters and glow gold.
